Built-in States

Pick the animation that plays in each situation. The overlay switches automatically: Active while you work, Idle/Sleeping when away, Watching when you hover, Sitting on the taskbar. Unassigned states fall back to Idle.

Custom States

Make your own state, give it an animation, and have it play whenever an app is open (e.g. code, chrome, spotify). Leave the app blank to trigger it by hand from the buddy's right-click menu.

Scene Builder

Build a living-wallpaper scene: a background (becomes your desktop wallpaper), a perspective floor your character walks, foreground props it walks behind, and anchors it visits — randomly or on a timer.
