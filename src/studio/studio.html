Built-in States

Pick the animation that plays in each situation. The overlay switches automatically: Active while you work, Idle/Sleeping when away, Watching when you hover, Sitting on the taskbar. Unassigned states fall back to Idle.

Custom States

Make your own state, give it an animation, and have it play whenever an app is open (e.g. code, chrome, spotify). Leave the app blank to trigger it by hand from the buddy's right-click menu.

Scene Builder

Build a living-wallpaper scene: a background (becomes your desktop wallpaper), a perspective floor your character walks, foreground props it walks behind, and anchors it visits — randomly or on a timer.

Scroll to zoom in/out · drag floor corners ◻ (can go far past the image when zoomed out), anchors ●, props ▭, or lights ☀ · click empty floor to add an anchor

Foreground Props

Images placed on the floor — the character walks in front of nearer ones and behind farther ones. Make a prop an anchor to have the character visit it and play an animation.

Anchors

Walls

Vertical surfaces the character walks around and that catch its shadow. Use the ▥ Wall tool to place one (click two floor points); switch to ✥ Select to pick a wall, set its height, or delete it. Drag ◆ ends / ▲ to reshape.

No walls yet.

No-walk Zones

Areas the character won't enter — it treats their edge as the end of the floor. Use the ⬣ No-walk tool to draw one (click corners, click the first point to close); switch to ✥ Select to pick a zone or delete it.

No zones yet.

Doors (entry)

Marks where the character enters this screen. With multiple screens it walks in through the door when crossing over, and the scene starts there. Use the 🚪 Door tool to place one per screen; Select to move or delete it.
