The story so far

About DeskBuddy

Every desktop pet we tried was a mascot on rails — a cute character that sits on your screen and does what it's told. Every living wallpaper was a pretty picture with nobody home. We wanted the thing in between: a companion that lives in a world you build.

So DeskBuddy is both. A character that wanders your desktop, and scenes with perspective floors, walls and doors that become your wallpaper — across every monitor you own. Import your own models, animate them with Mixamo clips, design their rooms, and watch them commute between your screens through a portal (because why not).

It's free, and creation will always be free — the plan is a small one-time Pro unlock for power features and a marketplace where creators earn by selling characters, scenes and animation packs. No subscriptions. Gatekeeping is for wallpapers.

The studio

DeskBuddy is the flagship project of Yuvexel — a small independent studio. More things are coming; this little paw is the first.

Say hi

Questions, ideas, bug reports — the support page goes straight to us and we actually read it. If you build something cool with DeskBuddy, we genuinely want to see it.
