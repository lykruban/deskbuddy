What it does

A pet, a world, and the tools to make both

Most desktop pets give you one character to sit on your screen. DeskBuddy gives you a world to build and a companion that lives in it.

🐾

Buddy mode

A character that hangs out on your desktop — wanders, idles, reacts, and keeps you company while you work.

🖼️

Living-wallpaper scenes

Drop your character into a built scene — perspective floors, walls, props and lighting — that becomes your wallpaper.

🖥️

Multi-monitor worlds

Each monitor becomes its own room. Your buddy walks — and teleports through doors — between screens.

🎨

Character Studio

Import your own 3D models and Mixamo animations, rig them, and make them yours. Creation is always free.

🧩

Scene Editor

Design rooms with walls, doors, no-walk zones, anchors and lighting — then watch your buddy live in them.

🛍️

MarketplaceComing soon

Discover, share and sell characters, animation packs and scenes. Built by the community, for the community.
