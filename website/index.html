What it does

A pet, a world, and the tools to make both

Most desktop pets give you one character to sit on your screen. We, being extra, gave you a whole world to build and a companion to live in it.

🐾

Buddy mode

A character that hangs out on your desktop — wanders, idles, reacts, and is suspiciously good company for something made of polygons.

🖼️

Living-wallpaper scenes

Drop your character into a real built scene — perspective floors, walls, props, lighting — that becomes your wallpaper. Take that, mountain.jpg.

Multi-monitor worlds

Each monitor becomes its own room. Your buddy walks — and teleports through doors — between screens. Yes, we made commuting cute.

Character Studio

Import your own 3D models and Mixamo animations and make them yours. Creation is always free, because gatekeeping is for wallpapers.

Scene Editor

Design rooms with walls, doors, no-walk zones, anchors and lighting — then watch your buddy actually live in them.
